01 — Project Overview

A large-scale park, modeled across complex terrain.

Royal Meadows is a large-scale park development. The work centered on advanced Revit modeling, reconciling complex site contours across multiple landscape zones with full Architectural/Civil coordination.

02 — The Challenge

Terrain that doesn't follow a grid.

Park-scale landscape modeling means working with continuously varying site contours rather than the regular geometry of a building — and coordinating that terrain model accurately against civil engineering data across multiple distinct landscape zones.

03 — Design Approach

Site-contour modeling, zone by zone.

Advanced Revit modeling techniques were used to reconcile intricate site contours with the architectural and landscape requirements of each zone, coordinated directly against civil engineering information.

04 — BIM Workflow

Site Survey Data→Revit Terrain Model→ Landscape Zoning→Arc/Civil Coordination→ Documentation
