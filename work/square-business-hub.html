01 — Project Overview

A commercial hub taken through the full BIM lifecycle.

Square Business Hub is a 10,000 m² commercial development in New Damietta, Egypt, delivered in 2025. The brief called for one continuous workflow — from concept through to a statutory-approved, construction-ready information set — rather than separate design and documentation phases.

02 — The Challenge

One model, every deliverable.

A commercial hub of this scale needs shop drawings, an accurate bill of quantities and a statutory municipal licensing package — each normally produced by a different process, often out of sync with the design. The challenge was keeping design, documentation and procurement data aligned as the project moved from concept to construction.

03 — Design Approach

Model-first, documentation second.

The commercial massing and facade were developed directly in a coordinated Revit model, so every downstream deliverable — shop drawings, quantities, licensing drawings — could be generated from the same source rather than redrawn.

04 — BIM Workflow

Concept→Revit Model→ Documentation→Coordination→ Clash Detection→BOQ / Schedules→ Construction Documentation
