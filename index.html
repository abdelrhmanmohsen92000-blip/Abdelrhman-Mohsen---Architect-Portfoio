09 About

Architecture, built on information.

BasedEgypt — open to relocation
AvailabilityGMT+2
RegistrationSCE-Eligible
LanguagesArabic · English

I work at the point where architectural intent has to survive contact with construction — translating design decisions into coordinated, buildable Revit models at LOD 350 and above, and keeping architecture, structure and MEP disciplines talking to each other before conflicts reach site.

My background spans full BIM lifecycles: from master planning and facade design, through clash-free multidisciplinary coordination, to construction-ready shop drawings, automated BOQ extraction and statutory municipal licensing. I've led that process across EDEC, Al-ORABI Engineering Consulting, VISION INTERNATIONAL GROUP and DIZ Studio, and handle it end-to-end for private clients through independent practice.

I hold a Bachelor of Architecture from Mansoura University and an Architectural BIM Diploma from KAITECH Academy, and I'm eligible for Saudi Council of Engineers (SCE) registration.
